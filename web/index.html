Formations & Tactics

Purple Tactical Board Every formation is read as a pitch shape: width, midfield density, back-line security, front-line pressure, compactness and vertical gaps. Tactical style then modifies how that shape creates xG, converts chances, protects transitions and handles late-game pressure.
Formation ShapeBack line, front line, midfield density, width, central presence and vertical gaps all affect Attack, Defense and transition quality.
ChemistryNatural positions, balanced lines and fewer OOP picks raise the team multiplier before match events are sampled.
Star MomentsGame Changers can create late pressure in finals, extra time, penalty rounds and matches after minute 80.
Creation vs ConversionMidfield creates raw xG volume; strikers decide how many go in. Low-xG wins now exist.
Style SynergyFormation + style fit gives a visible bonus (~+1.8–3.2% Attack/Defense) before the match kicks off.

Tactical Styles (9 — two new added: Direct & Tiki-Taka)

Direct Play NEWLong balls, target men, bypass the midfield. Elite finishers feast; bad midfield is hidden. Bad vs deep compact blocks.
Counter AttackAbsorb pressure then sprint speed on breaks. Good vs Gegenpress & Possession; weak vs Defensive low-blocks that deny space.
DefensiveGood vs Counter & Direct play. Weak vs Possession & Wing Play because deep blocks get stretched.
BalancedStable baseline, no sharp weakness or upside. Best when your XI is simply stronger and you want low-volatility results.
Compact MidfieldGood vs Gegenpress & Counter by closing central lanes. Weak vs Wing Play & Possession circulation.
GegenpressGood vs Possession & Tiki-Taka (force turnovers). Weak vs Counter & Compact teams that survive the first press.
Wing PlayGood vs Defensive & Compact via width. Weak vs Possession & presses that trap the wide lanes.
PossessionGood vs Defensive & Compact by territory. Weak vs Gegenpress & fast counters. Creation-heavy, conversion-light.
Tiki-Taka NEWShort triangles, patient, 500+ passes per game. Dominant creation but single-chance conversion suffers. Weak vs Gegenpress swarm.

Style Matchup Matrix

DefensiveGood vs Counter and Direct because space is denied. Bad vs Possession, Tiki-Taka and Wing Play because deep blocks get stretched or circulated around.
GegenpressGood vs Possession and Tiki-Taka by forcing high turnovers. Bad vs Counter and Compact when the first press is survived.
PossessionGood vs Defensive and Compact through territory control. Bad vs Gegenpress and Counter because turnovers are punished.
Counter AttackGood vs Gegenpress, Possession and Tiki-Taka. Bad vs Defensive or Direct teams that do not leave useful space.
Wing PlayGood vs Defensive and Compact by overloading wide lanes. Bad vs Possession and Gegenpress when wide outlets are trapped.
Compact MidfieldGood vs Gegenpress, Counter and Direct by winning second balls. Bad vs Wing Play and patient Possession.
Direct PlayGood vs slow creation-heavy teams with weak forwards. Bad vs Defensive, Compact and Wing Play because long balls become predictable.
Tiki-TakaGood vs passive Defensive teams through repeated creation. Bad vs Gegenpress, Counter and Direct pressure on turnovers.
BalancedNo hard counter. It protects squad quality when your XI is stronger and you do not want high tactical volatility.

Two-phase xG: Created xG vs Converted xG

Creation phase (Midfield) — volume of chances. Raised by Tiki-Taka, Possession, Gegenpress, Compact + 5-midfielder formations. Conversion phase (Strikers) — how many chances → goals. Raised by Direct, Counter, Wing Play, 2–4 striker formations & STs rated 86+. Realism now: low xG teams can absolutely win. 4-2-4 + Direct with a pair of 88-rated strikers outperforms their xG; a beautiful-passing Tiki-Taka team with a 76-rated ST will underperform theirs. Synergy bonus shows in the match preview — 4-2-3-1 + Possession is a great fit; 5-4-1 + Tiki-Taka is wasted.

Custom Tactics

Personal saved styles - tactics built in Engine Lab appear in the post-draft style picker under Saved. Only the owner can lock and use them. Live trade-offs - sliders show Attack, Defense, Control, xG Creation, Finishing, Transition, Risk, Discipline, Set Pieces and Star Moment changes before saving. Engine impact - saved values feed the same match simulator fields as built-in styles, including xG split, card risk, star moments and set-piece pressure. Use case - build one tactic for elite midfield creators, one for clinical strikers, and one safer knockout tactic for protecting a lead.

Formation Reading

Wide outlets improve crossing and stretch compact blocks. Midfield density improves control, second balls and defensive cover. Large vertical gaps hurt chemistry and make transitions less reliable. High front lines raise pressure and shot volume but increase defensive risk. Formations with ≥5 CMs (3-5-2, 4-3-2-1, 2-5-2-1) pair with Possession/Tiki-Taka; formations with 3–4 forwards (4-2-4, 3-4-3, 5-2-3) pair with Direct/Counter.

Rooms & Settings

Create a room to get a 6-character invite code, or join one with a friend's code, or jump straight into Single Player against 47 bots. The room creator configures:

  • Human slots — up to 32 real players in one room.
  • Pick timer — 10s / 20s / 30s / 45s per draft pick, or No Limit to disable the countdown entirely. Run out (in a timed room) and the server auto-picks the best available player for you.
  • Show Ratings — turn off for a "blind" draft where overalls are hidden entirely.
  • Team Captain — if on, everyone must choose a captain (+6 overall, applied before position weighting) once their draft is complete.
  • Tournament Length — Off (full 48-team group stage), Blitz (skip straight to the Round of 32), or Top 8 (skip straight to the Quarter-Finals, 1/4). Every human is guaranteed a spot in a shortened bracket; bots are randomly trimmed to fill the rest.
  • Restrict Draft Teams (optional) — the creator can limit which nations are eligible to be revealed during the draft to a hand-picked list of at least 4 teams, grouped by confederation for easy browsing. Leave it empty for the default: all 48 teams eligible.
  • Rerolls — Off, 1, 2, or 3 per drafter. A reroll skips the currently revealed team for a brand new one without picking from it — that skipped team still counts as seen and won't come back around later.
  • Experimental Auto Draft - Single Player only. When enabled, the engine drafts your full XI for the locked formation, picks the highest-rated captain when needed, locks a tactical style, and starts the tournament automatically.
  • Spoiler Mode - multiplayer-only. If enabled, real players advance stage by stage together. Each player marks the current stage as ready; once every required player is checked in, the next round unlocks. If your team is eliminated, later results stay hidden until the tournament is fully finished, even if no human teams remain alive.

Once every member finishes drafting (multiplayer rooms), the tournament doesn't start automatically — the room creator confirms it with a "Start Tournament" button, so nobody gets pulled into the World Cup mid-draft. Singleplayer starts immediately since there's nobody else to wait for.

The Draft

Each round a random real 2026 World Cup team is revealed with its actual squad — every player in the game is a real 2026 squad member, no fictional players anywhere. A team is never revealed to you twice — every eligible nation shows up at most once over the course of your draft. Pick one available player — a position already full for you, or a player someone else in the room already grabbed, is greyed out. All 1,248 real players sit in one pool shared by the whole room, so a player you want can be sniped before your next reveal. If a room restricts draft teams, only that list is eligible — otherwise every eligible nation reveals independently to each drafter, so two people revealed the same country at once simply pick from that team's available squad separately, exactly like any other reveal. If a slot's position group has more than one open slot (say two open center-back spots), you choose exactly which one your pick fills. Use the sort icons above the player grid to reorder it by rating, position, or shuffle it randomly.

Your Profile controls the Draft screen layout. Pitch First puts your squad map above the player list, which is the recommended mobile setup; Vertical and Side-by-Side remain available for laptop play. Formation is locked once the draft starts, so the shape you chose before entering draft cannot change. You can still tap a filled pitch slot and then another compatible slot in the same position group to move or swap players before locking the tactical style.

Match Engine

Every match — bot vs bot, human vs bot, human vs human — comes from the same engine:

  • Attack / Defense ratings - a weighted average of your 11 players' overalls, weighted by how advanced or deep their slot sits. The player-quality curve is stronger now, so the gap between 70, 80 and 90 overall players is visible in ratings, goals, assists, saves and penalties.
  • Chemistry - a multiplier built from linkage, balanced squad quality, Game Changer leadership, correct position fit, line balance across defense/midfield/attack, and penalties for out-of-position picks. Bot squads are one real nation, so they keep perfect linkage; human all-star squads must manage fit and balance.
  • Tactical Style - after the draft, each team locks a style (9 available: 2 new styles added — Direct Play and Tiki-Taka). Every style has modifiers for Attack, Defense, Tempo, Risk, Press, Control, Transition, Set Pieces and Star Moment, plus a Midfield Creation Bias × Finisher Conversion Bias pair (see below). Matchups matter: Defensive beats Counter but struggles vs Possession; Gegenpress beats Possession/Tiki-Taka but is vulnerable to Counter; Possession beats Defensive but struggles vs pressing/counters; Counter beats Gegenpress and Possession but struggles vs Defensive; Wing Play beats Defensive/Compact but struggles vs Possession/Gegenpress; Compact beats Gegenpress/Counter but struggles vs Wing Play/Possession; Direct beats creation-heavy teams with bad finishers but struggles vs compact deep blocks; Tiki-Taka dominates possession vs passive teams but collapses vs gegenpress swarms; Balanced has no major weakness.
  • Two-phase xG (Creation vs Conversion) — xG is now calculated in two independent passes. First, your midfielders + tactical style generate chance creation volume (Tiki-Taka, Possession, Gegenpress and ≥5-CM formations all amplify this). Then your top finishers' quality + style decide conversion rate (Direct, Counter, 4-2-4, and 86+ rated STs/CFs strongly amplify this). It is now entirely possible to win with low total xG: a bad midfield paired with Direct + elite strikers will generate fewer chances but convert far more of the ones they get. The opposite also applies (great midfield + bad striker = lots of xG, few goals).
  • Formation + Style Synergy — a visible bonus/penalty (~+1.8 to +3.2% Attack / matching Defense range) applied before xG is sampled if your tactical style fits (or clashes with) your chosen formation. 4-2-3-1 + Possession is "Great fit"; 5-4-1 + Tiki-Taka is "Poor fit" (shown on the style picker cards).
  • Morale Dynamic - recent results carry into the next match as persistent morale. On Matchday 3, qualification pressure is calculated from current rank, points and goal difference; must-win teams receive a controlled urgency/composure lift and a small volatility increase before xG is sampled.
  • Star Moments - starred Game Changers can create a special late chance after minute 80, in finals, extra time or pressure penalty rounds. These moments add real xG before the score is sampled, so they are part of the engine rather than cosmetic text.
  • Human vs AI Boost - human-controlled teams play at 1.2x Attack and Defense only when facing an AI team. Human vs human and AI vs AI matches stay neutral.
  • Formation Edge — attacking shape vs. the opponent's defensive shape, plus a width mismatch bonus.
  • Captain Bonus — a designated captain's overall is boosted before all of the above.
  • Red Card Morale Hit — a player sent off rattles their own team for a short spell afterward, regardless of that player's individual quality: a temporary Attack + Defense reduction. Losing the captain hits harder — the affected spell runs 1.8x longer.

Goals are then sampled from a Poisson distribution around each side's "expected goals," so the better team wins more often - never automatically. The lineup reveal's Predict Chance now uses the same engine power model: weighted player overalls, chemistry, tactical style, Game Changer threat, formation fit and the 48-team field strength. It also projects likely scorer, creator and pressure player from the same role/quality weights used by live match events. Full formulas are in the downloadable Match Engine PDF.

World Cup Format

48 real teams, 12 random groups of 4, round-robin (3 matchdays). Top 2 of each group plus the best 8 third-placed teams (24 + 8 = 32) advance to the Round of 32 — the real FIFA 2026 format. The knockout draw separates same-group qualifiers into different early-path blocks, so a group winner and runner-up do not immediately meet again in Round of 32 or Round of 16. Then single elimination runs: Round of 32 → Round of 16 → Quarter-Finals → Semi-Finals → Final. Blitz Game rooms skip straight to Round of 32; Top 8 rooms skip straight to Quarter-Finals. A tied knockout match plays extra time before penalties.

Live Reports & Results

Results arrive one stage at a time — nobody is forced to watch 24 matches at once, and one person clicking "Continue" doesn't drag anyone else's screen forward unless Spoiler Mode is on, where every still-active human player must finish the current stage before the next one can be generated. Your own match plays out on a live ticking clock (goals, assists, cards) through extra time if needed, and a penalty shootout — if it comes to that — plays out kick by kick, not as an instant result. Every other match in that round is available as a static final report via the match picker. Every report also shows team performance stats — possession, shots (on target), passes and accuracy, corners, fouls, cards, saves, and xG — derived from the same Attack/Defense ratings that decided the match.

Elimination

If your team is knocked out, you're not removed from the room — you become a spectator for the rest of that World Cup, right through to the champion.

Tournament Awards

Once a champion is crowned, the results screen shows the winning user first when the champion is human, with the team name below it. The same page tallies the tournament into Golden Ball, Golden Boot, Creator Award, Golden Glove, Knockout Hero, and Heat Check. The full knockout route opens as a two-sided bracket chart: one half advances from the left, the other from the right, and the final lands in the center. Engine Match Review adds a post-match breakdown from xG, player quality, chemistry, morale pressure, tactical style, star moments, shot pressure, possession, passing, saves, cards, extra time and penalties; it explains why the match tilted without changing the score after the fact.
