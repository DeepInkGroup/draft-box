📖 Rulebook

Download full Match Engine PDF

This is not a fantasy league. Every draft round reveals one random real national team — you take exactly one player from it for your squad. Speed, luck, and managing which slots you still need are the whole game.

Rooms & Settings

Create a room to get a 6-character invite code, or join one with a friend's code, or jump straight into Single Player against 47 bots. The room creator configures:

  • Human slots — up to 32 real players in one room.
  • Pick timer — 10s / 20s / 30s / 60s per draft pick. Run out and the server auto-picks the best available player for you.
  • Show Ratings — turn off for a "blind" draft where overalls are hidden entirely.
  • Team Captain — if on, everyone must choose a captain (+6 overall, applied before position weighting) once their draft is complete.
  • Tournament Length — Off (full 48-team group stage), Blitz (skip straight to the Round of 32), or Top 8 (skip straight to the Quarter-Finals, 1/4). Every human is guaranteed a spot in a shortened bracket; bots are randomly trimmed to fill the rest.
  • Restrict Draft Teams (optional) — the creator can limit which nations are eligible to be revealed during the draft to a hand-picked list of at least 4 teams. Leave it empty for the default: all 48 teams eligible.

The Draft

Each round a random real 2026 World Cup team is revealed with its actual squad — every player in the game is a real 2026 squad member, no fictional players anywhere. Pick one available player — a position already full for you, or a player someone else in the room already grabbed, is greyed out. All 1,248 real players sit in one pool shared by the whole room, so a player you want can be sniped before your next reveal. If a room restricts draft teams, only that list is eligible — otherwise every eligible nation reveals independently to each drafter, so two people revealed the same country at once simply pick from that team's available squad separately, exactly like any other reveal. If a slot's position group has more than one open slot (say two open center-back spots), you choose exactly which one your pick fills.

Formations

16 formations to choose from (4-3-3, 4-4-2, 5-2-3, 3-2-4-1, 4-2-4, and more), each an explicit named layout — not just position counts — so you always know exactly which slot (LB vs RB, which CB, etc.) a pick will fill.

Match Engine

Every match — bot vs bot, human vs bot, human vs human — comes from the same engine:

  • Attack / Defense ratings — a weighted average of your 11 players' overalls, weighted by how advanced or deep their slot sits.
  • Chemistry — a 0.94-1.06 multiplier from three factors: Linkage (do neighboring players share a real source nation), Balance (consistent squad quality), and Leadership (real star players). Bot squads are one real nation, so they always have perfect Linkage — a deliberate trade-off against human "all-star" squads built from many countries.
  • Formation Edge — attacking shape vs. the opponent's defensive shape, plus a width mismatch bonus.
  • Captain Bonus — a designated captain's overall is boosted before all of the above.

Goals are then sampled from a Poisson distribution around each side's "expected goals," so the better team wins more often — never automatically. Full formulas are in the downloadable Match Engine PDF.

World Cup Format

48 real teams, 12 random groups of 4, round-robin (3 matchdays). Top 2 of each group plus the best 8 third-placed teams (24 + 8 = 32) advance to the Round of 32 — the real FIFA 2026 format. Then single elimination: Round of 32 → Round of 16 → Quarter-finals → Semi-finals → Final. Knockout draws go to penalties. Blitz Game rooms skip straight to the Round of 32; Top 8 rooms skip straight to the Quarter-Finals with only 8 teams.

Live Reports & Results

Results arrive one stage at a time — nobody is forced to watch 24 matches at once, and one person clicking "Continue" doesn't drag anyone else's screen forward. Your own match plays out on a live ticking clock (goals, assists, cards); every other match in that round is available as a static final report via the match picker.

Elimination

If your team is knocked out, you're not removed from the room — you become a spectator for the rest of that World Cup, right through to the champion.

Data

All 48 qualified nations and every player are real 2026 World Cup squad members (sourced from official squad lists). Overall ratings are gameplay values, not an official license.
