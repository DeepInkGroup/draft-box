A good XI plays together

Overall ratings are only part of your team's performance.

01

Use natural positions

Match players to their preferred roles. Position fit and balanced lines help your squad create chances and defend as a unit.

02

Connect your squad

Player links, leadership and squad balance contribute to chemistry. A mix of creators, finishers and defensive cover gives your plan a stronger foundation.

03

Allow time to settle

In League Games, new signings briefly reduce coordination. Chemistry returns over the next few matches as the new lineup settles in.

04

Read the match report

Look at chances, xG, possession and the match review alongside the score. Use those clues to decide where your squad or style needs help.

No perfect formula

Formation fit, tactical matchups and player quality all matter. Even a well-built team can lose a close match.
